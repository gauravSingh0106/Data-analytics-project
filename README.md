📌 Project Overview

Designed an interactive Power BI Sales Dashboard to analyze pizza sales performance, customer ordering behavior, product category trends, and revenue contribution across different dimensions. The dashboard helps business stakeholders identify sales opportunities, optimize inventory, and improve overall business performance through data-driven decision-making.


**Business Objective**

*-The objective of this project is to analyze pizza sales data and answer key business questions such as:

2-Which pizza category generates the highest revenue?
3-Which pizza size contributes the most to sales?
4-Which day and month receive the highest number of orders?
5-What is the overall business performance in terms of Revenue, Orders, Quantity Sold, and Average Order Value?
6-Which products should the business focus on to maximize revenue?


**Business Questions (KPIs)**
**Primary KPIs**
Total Revenue Generated
1-Total Orders
2-Total Quantity Sold
3-Average Order Value
4-Average Pizza Price
5-Maximum Pizza Price
6-Minimum Pizza Price
