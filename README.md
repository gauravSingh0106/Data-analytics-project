📌 Project Overview

Designed an interactive Power BI Sales Dashboard to analyze pizza sales performance, customer ordering behavior,<br> product category trends, and revenue contribution across different dimensions. The dashboard helps business stakeholders identify sales opportunities, optimize inventory, and improve overall business performance through data-driven decision-making.


**Business Objective**

*-The objective of this project is to analyze pizza sales data and answer key business questions such as:

2-Which pizza category generates the highest revenue?<br>
3-Which pizza size contributes the most to sales?<br>
4-Which day and month receive the highest number of orders?<br>
5-What is the overall business performance in terms of Revenue, Orders, Quantity Sold, and Average Order Value?<br>
6-Which products should the business focus on to maximize revenue?<br>
